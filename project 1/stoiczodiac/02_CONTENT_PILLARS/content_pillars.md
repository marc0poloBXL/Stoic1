## P3: Reel — Voiceover Wisdom (3x/week)

**Format**: Reel (15–30s, 1080×1920)  
**Goal**: High-reach, shareable video content

### Reel Structure
```
0:00–0:02  — Hook text: "♑ Capricorn, this is for you"
0:02–0:15  — Zen visuals + narrated stoic quote
0:15–0:20  — Application to modern life
0:20–0:25  — CTA overlay: "Follow @stoiczodiac for daily wisdom"
0:25–0:30  — Fade to black with sign icon
```

### Reel Types
| Type | Description | Audio |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **R1: Quote Narration** | Zen visuals + narrated quote | Calm male/female voice |
| **R2: Sign Comparison** | "Aries vs. Taurus: Stoic edition" | Voiceover + trending audio |
| **R3: Rapid Wisdom** | 3 stoic rules for 1 sign in 15s | Fast-paced, trending |
| **R4: Philosopher Speak** | "What Marcus Aurelius would say to your sign" | Deep voice, echo effect |

### Weekly Reel Schedule
- **Monday**: R1 — Quote Narration
- **Wednesday**: R2 — Sign Comparison or R4 — Philosopher Speaks
- **Friday**: R3 — Rapid Wisdom

> **💡 Reel sign alignment**: The calendar generator assigns Reels on fixed weekdays
> (Mon/Wed/Fri) independently of which sign gets the daily post. This means your
> Monday Reel sign may differ from Monday's daily quote sign. That's fine for reach
> (Reels attract a different audience segment), but if you prefer tighter thematic
> alignment, manually match the Reel sign to the day's featured sign when editing.
