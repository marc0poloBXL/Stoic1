# Scheduling Setup Guide — Later (Free Tier)

**Tool**: Later (free tier — 30 posts/month)  
**Alternative**: Buffer (free tier — 10 posts)  
**Caveat**: Free tier doesn't support Reels scheduling — post Reels manually

---

## ✅ Step 1: Account Setup

1. Go to [later.com](https://later.com) → Sign up (free)
2. Connect Instagram account
3. Grant permissions (read + publish)

---

## ✅ Step 2: Content Upload Strategy

Free tier = 30 posts/month = 1 post/day (perfect for daily quotes)

### What to Schedule
| Content | Schedule via Later? | Why |
|---------|-------------------|-----|
| Daily Quote (image) | ✅ Yes | 1/day = 30/month = fits free tier |
| Carousels | ✅ Yes | Counts as 1 post |
| Stories | ❌ No | Post manually (free tier limit) |
| Reels | ❌ No | Post manually (free tier limit) |

### This Means
- **Later**: Schedule all 30 daily quote images
- **Manual**: Post Reels (3x/week), Carousels (3x/week), Stories (daily)

## ✅ Step 4: Weekly Scheduling Routine

### Sundays: Batch + Schedule
```
[ ] 1. Generate 7 quotes (ChatGPT) — 10 min
[ ] 2. Generate 7 captions (ChatGPT) — 10 min
[ ] 3. Design 7 quote images (Canva batch) — 30 min
[ ] 4. Upload 7 images to Later — 5 min
[ ] 5. Write captions + hashtags in Later — 15 min
[ ] 6. Set posting times — 2 min
[ ] 7. Edit 3 Reels in CapCut — 45 min
[ ] 8. Write 7 story prompts — 10 min
     Total: ~2 hours
```
