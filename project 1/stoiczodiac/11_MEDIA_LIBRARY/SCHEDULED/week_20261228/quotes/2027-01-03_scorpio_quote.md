# ♏ Scorpio — Day 140
**Theme**: The depths of character
**Philosopher**: Zeno
**Source**: Zeno of Citium

## Quote
> "When a dog is tied to a cart, if it wants to follow, it is pulled and follows, making its act coincide with necessity. But if it does not want to follow, it will be compelled in any case."

## Application
For Scorpio, whose element shapes how you meet the world. The lesson isn't in what you do, but in how you choose to see it. This week, let this truth settle into your daily rhythm.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Scorpio, act as if your character — not your reputation — is the only thing that matters.
