# ♐ Sagittarius — Day 51
**Theme**: Aiming with wisdom
**Philosopher**: Musonius Rufus
**Source**: Lectures, 4

## Quote
> "Virtue is the only true good, and vice the only true evil."

## Application
As a Sagittarius, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Musonius Rufus reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Sagittarius: do one small thing not for applause, but because it's right.
