# ♏ Scorpio — Day 50
**Theme**: The depths of character
**Philosopher**: Zeno
**Source**: Zeno of Citium

## Quote
> "No one entrusts a secret to a drunken man; but one entrusts a secret to a good man. So goodness is stronger than wine."

## Application
For Scorpio, whose element drives you to lead with passion, this wisdom cuts through the noise. Let Zeno's words ground your natural fire — the lesson isn't in what you do, but in how you choose to see it. This week, let this truth settle into your daily rhythm.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Scorpio, act as if your character — not your reputation — is the only thing that matters.
