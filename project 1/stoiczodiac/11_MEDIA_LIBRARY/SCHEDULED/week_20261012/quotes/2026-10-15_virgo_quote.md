# ♍ Virgo — Day 60
**Theme**: The art of enough
**Philosopher**: Seneca
**Source**: Letters from a Stoic, II

## Quote
> "It is not the man who has too little, but the man who craves more, that is poor."

## Application
Virgo energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Seneca offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Virgo, notice where you're seeking approval — and give it to yourself instead.
