# ♍ Virgo — Day 6
**Theme**: Perfection in imperfection
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Enchiridion, 5

## Quote
> "People are not disturbed by things, but by the views they take of them."

## Application
Virgo energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Epictetus offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Virgo: do one small thing not for applause, but because it's right.
