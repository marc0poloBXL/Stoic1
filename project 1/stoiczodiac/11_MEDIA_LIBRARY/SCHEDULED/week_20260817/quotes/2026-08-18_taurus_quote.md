# ♉ Taurus — Day 2
**Theme**: Letting go of attachment
**Philosopher**: Seneca
**Source**: Letters from a Stoic, LXV

## Quote
> "What fortune has made yours is not your own."

## Application
As a Taurus, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Seneca reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Taurus, your challenge today: notice one thing you've been clinging to, and loosen your grip.
