# ♈ Aries — Day 1
**Theme**: Channeling impulse into purpose
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 10.16

## Quote
> "Waste no more time arguing what a good man should be. Be one."

## Application
Aries energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Marcus Aurelius offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Aries, before noon, write down one fear. Then ask: is this real, or imagined?
