# ♌ Leo — Day 5
**Theme**: Humility within greatness
**Philosopher**: Seneca
**Source**: Letters from a Stoic, LXXXIV

## Quote
> "It is a rough road that leads to the heights of greatness."

## Application
As a Leo, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Seneca reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Leo, before noon, write down one fear. Then ask: is this real, or imagined?
