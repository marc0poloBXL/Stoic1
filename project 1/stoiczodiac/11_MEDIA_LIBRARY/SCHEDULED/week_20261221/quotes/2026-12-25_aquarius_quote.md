# ♒ Aquarius — Day 131
**Theme**: The examined life
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Discourses, 1.6

## Quote
> "What would have become of Hercules if there had been no lion, no hydra, no boar? He would have remained unknown."

## Application
As a Aquarius, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Epictetus reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Aquarius: pause once before reacting. Ask yourself — is this in my control?
