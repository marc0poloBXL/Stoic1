# ♈ Aries — Day 133
**Theme**: Temper your fire
**Philosopher**: Musonius Rufus
**Source**: Lectures, 9

## Quote
> "We should train ourselves not to be easily upset by the misfortunes that happen to us."

## Application
For Aries, whose element shapes how you meet the world. The lesson isn't in what you do, but in how you choose to see it. This week, let this truth settle into your daily rhythm.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Aries, notice where you're seeking approval — and give it to yourself instead.
