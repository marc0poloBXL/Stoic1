# ♐ Sagittarius — Day 21
**Theme**: The disciplined adventurer
**Philosopher**: Seneca

## Quote

## Application
As a Sagittarius, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Seneca reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Sagittarius: pause once before reacting. Ask yourself — is this in my control?
