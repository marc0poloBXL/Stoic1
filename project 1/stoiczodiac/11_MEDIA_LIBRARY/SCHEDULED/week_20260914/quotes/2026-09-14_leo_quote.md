# ♌ Leo — Day 29
**Theme**: True worth
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Enchiridion, 5

## Quote
> "People are not disturbed by things, but by the views they take of them."

## Application
As a Leo, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Epictetus reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Leo, act as if your character — not your reputation — is the only thing that matters.
