# ♌ Leo — Day 35
**Theme**: Strength in service
**Philosopher**: Musonius Rufus
**Source**: Lectures, 13

## Quote
> "A human being's highest purpose is to serve others with wisdom and justice."

## Application
As a Leo, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Musonius Rufus reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Leo: pause once before reacting. Ask yourself — is this in my control?
