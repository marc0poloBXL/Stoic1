# ♏ Scorpio — Day 110
**Theme**: The obstacle is the way
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 8.49

## Quote
> "Everything we hear is an opinion, not a fact. Everything we see is a perspective, not the truth."

## Application
As a Scorpio, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Marcus Aurelius reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Scorpio: pause once before reacting. Ask yourself — is this in my control?
