# ♎ Libra — Day 109
**Theme**: The peace of right action
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Discourses, 3.22

## Quote
> "There is only one way to happiness — cease worrying about things which are beyond the power of our will."

## Application
Libra energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Epictetus offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Libra, your challenge today: notice one thing you've been clinging to, and loosen your grip.
