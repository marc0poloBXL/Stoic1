# ♒ Aquarius — Day 65
**Theme**: The world as one
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 5.16

## Quote
> "The soul becomes dyed with the color of its thoughts."

## Application
As a Aquarius, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Marcus Aurelius reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Aquarius, before noon, write down one fear. Then ask: is this real, or imagined?
