# ♋ Cancer — Day 70
**Theme**: The open hand
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 6.2

## Quote
> "You have power over your mind — not outside events. Realize this, and you will find strength."

## Application
As a Cancer, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Marcus Aurelius reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Cancer, before noon, write down one fear. Then ask: is this real, or imagined?
