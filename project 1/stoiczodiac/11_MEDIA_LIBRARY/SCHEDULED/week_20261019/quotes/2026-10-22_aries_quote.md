# ♈ Aries — Day 67
**Theme**: Reckoning with patience
**Philosopher**: Seneca
**Source**: On Anger, 1.1

## Quote
> "Anger, if not restrained, is frequently more hurtful to us than the injury that provokes it."

## Application
Aries energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Seneca offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Aries, try this: when frustration rises, take three breaths before speaking.
