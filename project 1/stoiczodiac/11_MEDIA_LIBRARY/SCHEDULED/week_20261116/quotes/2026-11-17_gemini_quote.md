# ♊ Gemini — Day 93
**Theme**: Mastery of the inner dialogue
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Discourses, 1.14

## Quote
> "When you close your doors and make darkness within, remember never to say that you are alone. You are not alone; God is within."

## Application
Gemini energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Epictetus offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Gemini: do one small thing not for applause, but because it's right.
