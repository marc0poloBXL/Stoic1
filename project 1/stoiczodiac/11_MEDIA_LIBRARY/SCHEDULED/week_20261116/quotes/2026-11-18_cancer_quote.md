# ♋ Cancer — Day 94
**Theme**: Strength through vulnerability
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 7.67

## Quote
> "Very little is needed to make a happy life; it is all within yourself, in your way of thinking."

## Application
Cancer energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Marcus Aurelius offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Cancer, notice where you're seeking approval — and give it to yourself instead.
