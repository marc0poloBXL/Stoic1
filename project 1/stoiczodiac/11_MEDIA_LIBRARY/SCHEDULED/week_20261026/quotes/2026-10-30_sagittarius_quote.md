# ♐ Sagittarius — Day 75
**Theme**: The disciplined dreamer
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Discourses, 4.1

## Quote
> "Freedom is the only worthy goal in life. It is won by disregarding things that lie beyond our control."

## Application
For Sagittarius, whose element drives you to lead with passion, this wisdom cuts through the noise. Let Epictetus's words ground your natural fire — the lesson isn't in what you do, but in how you choose to see it. This week, let this truth settle into your daily rhythm.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Sagittarius: pause once before reacting. Ask yourself — is this in my control?
