# ♏ Scorpio — Day 74
**Theme**: Letting go
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 5.2

## Quote
> "The happiness of your life depends upon the quality of your thoughts."

## Application
As a Scorpio, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Marcus Aurelius reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Scorpio, act as if your character — not your reputation — is the only thing that matters.
