# ♍ Virgo — Day 72
**Theme**: The practical idealist
**Philosopher**: Musonius Rufus
**Source**: Lectures, 6

## Quote
> "If you pursue the good, you will become good. If you pursue the bad, you will become bad."

## Application
Virgo energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Musonius Rufus offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Virgo, act as if your character — not your reputation — is the only thing that matters.
