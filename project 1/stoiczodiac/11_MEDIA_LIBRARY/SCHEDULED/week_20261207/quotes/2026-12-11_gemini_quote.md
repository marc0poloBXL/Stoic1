# ♊ Gemini — Day 117
**Theme**: The power of stillness
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 6.52

## Quote
> "You always own the option of having no opinion. There is never any need to get worked up."

## Application
As a Gemini, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Marcus Aurelius reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Gemini, your challenge today: notice one thing you've been clinging to, and loosen your grip.
