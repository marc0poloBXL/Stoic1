# ♒ Aquarius — Day 113
**Theme**: The common good
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 10.5

## Quote

## Daily Challenge
Aquarius, your challenge today: notice one thing you've been clinging to, and loosen your grip.
