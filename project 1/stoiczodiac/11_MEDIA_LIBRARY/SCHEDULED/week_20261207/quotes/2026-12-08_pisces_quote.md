# ♓ Pisces — Day 114
**Theme**: The courage to let go
**Philosopher**: Seneca
**Source**: Letters from a Stoic, CIV

## Quote
> "It's not because things are difficult that we dare not; it's because we dare not that they are difficult."

## Application
Pisces energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Seneca offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Pisces, before noon, write down one fear. Then ask: is this real, or imagined?
