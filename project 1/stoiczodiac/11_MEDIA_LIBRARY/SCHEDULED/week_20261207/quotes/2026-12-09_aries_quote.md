# ♈ Aries — Day 115
**Theme**: Choosing your battles
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Discourses, 3.16

## Quote
> "The key is to keep company only with people who uplift you, whose presence calls forth your best."

## Application
For Aries, whose element shapes how you meet the world. The lesson isn't in what you do, but in how you choose to see it. This week, let this truth settle into your daily rhythm.

## Daily Challenge
