# ♈ Aries — Day 13
**Theme**: Restraint as strength
**Philosopher**: Seneca
**Source**: Letters from a Stoic, XXXVII

## Quote
> "He who is brave is free."

## Application
As a Aries, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Seneca reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Aries: pause once before reacting. Ask yourself — is this in my control?
