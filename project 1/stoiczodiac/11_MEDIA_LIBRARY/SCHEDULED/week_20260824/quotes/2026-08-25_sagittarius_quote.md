# ♐ Sagittarius — Day 9
**Theme**: Freedom through discipline
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Discourses, 4.1

## Quote
> "Freedom is the only worthy goal in life. It is won by disregarding things that lie beyond our control."

## Application
Sagittarius energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Epictetus offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Sagittarius: pause once before reacting. Ask yourself — is this in my control?
