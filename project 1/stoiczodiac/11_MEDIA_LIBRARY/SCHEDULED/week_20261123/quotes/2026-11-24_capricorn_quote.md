# ♑ Capricorn — Day 100
**Theme**: Ambition aligned with virtue
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 9.31

## Quote
> "To live a good life: We have the potential for it. If we can learn to be indifferent to what makes no difference."

## Application
Capricorn energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Marcus Aurelius offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Capricorn, try this: when frustration rises, take three breaths before speaking.
