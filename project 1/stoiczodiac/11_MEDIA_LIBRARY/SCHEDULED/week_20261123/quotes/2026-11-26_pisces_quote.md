# ♓ Pisces — Day 102
**Theme**: Boundaries of the compassionate soul
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Discourses, 3.24

## Quote
> "He is a wise man who does not grieve for the things which he has not, but rejoices for those which he has."

## Application
As a Pisces, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Epictetus reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Pisces, before noon, write down one fear. Then ask: is this real, or imagined?
