# ♓ Pisces — Day 42
**Theme**: The boundless self
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 7.59

## Quote
> "Look within. Within is the fountain of good, and it will ever bubble up, if you will ever dig."

## Application
Pisces energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Marcus Aurelius offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Pisces, notice where you're seeking approval — and give it to yourself instead.
