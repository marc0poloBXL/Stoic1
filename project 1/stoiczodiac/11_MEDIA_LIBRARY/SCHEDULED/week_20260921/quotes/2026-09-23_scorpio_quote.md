# ♏ Scorpio — Day 38
**Theme**: Mastery of desire
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Discourses, 1.29

## Quote
> "Who is your master? Anyone who has power over things you desire or avoid."

## Application
As a Scorpio, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Epictetus reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Scorpio: pause once before reacting. Ask yourself — is this in my control?
