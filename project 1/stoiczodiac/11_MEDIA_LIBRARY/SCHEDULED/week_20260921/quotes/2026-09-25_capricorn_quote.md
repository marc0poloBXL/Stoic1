# ♑ Capricorn — Day 40
**Theme**: Legacy over status
**Philosopher**: Seneca
**Source**: Letters from a Stoic, XIII

## Quote
> "We suffer more often in imagination than in reality."

## Application
As a Capricorn, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Seneca reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Capricorn, notice where you're seeking approval — and give it to yourself instead.
