# ♓ Pisces — Day 78
**Theme**: The empathetic boundary
**Philosopher**: Musonius Rufus
**Source**: Lectures, 6

## Quote
> "If you pursue the good, you will become good. If you pursue the bad, you will become bad."

## Application
As a Pisces, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Musonius Rufus reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Pisces, before noon, write down one fear. Then ask: is this real, or imagined?
