# ♊ Gemini — Day 81
**Theme**: Words that heal
**Philosopher**: Musonius Rufus
**Source**: Lectures, 3

## Quote
> "The philosopher is a physician of the soul, not a maker of fine speeches."

## Application
As a Gemini, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Musonius Rufus reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Gemini, before noon, write down one fear. Then ask: is this real, or imagined?
