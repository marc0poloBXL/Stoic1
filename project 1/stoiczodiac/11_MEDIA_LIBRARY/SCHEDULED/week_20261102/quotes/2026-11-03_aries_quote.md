# ♈ Aries — Day 79
**Theme**: Action with wisdom
**Philosopher**: Zeno
**Source**: Zeno of Citium

## Quote
> "We have two ears and one mouth, so we should listen more than we say."

## Application
Aries energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Zeno offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Aries, your challenge today: notice one thing you've been clinging to, and loosen your grip.
