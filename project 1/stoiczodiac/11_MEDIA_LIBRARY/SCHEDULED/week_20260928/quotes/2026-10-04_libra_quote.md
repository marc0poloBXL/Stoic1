# ♎ Libra — Day 49
**Theme**: Harmony in action
**Philosopher**: Musonius Rufus
**Source**: Lectures, 17

## Quote
> "The goal of life is to live in accordance with nature, which means living virtuously."

## Application
Libra energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Musonius Rufus offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Libra, before noon, write down one fear. Then ask: is this real, or imagined?
