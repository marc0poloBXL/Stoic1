# ♉ Taurus — Day 44
**Theme**: Rooted in virtue
**Philosopher**: Zeno
**Source**: Zeno of Citium

## Quote
> "Virtue is the only good. Everything else is indifferent."

## Application
As a Taurus, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Zeno reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Taurus, act as if your character — not your reputation — is the only thing that matters.
