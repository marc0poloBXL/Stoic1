# ♈ Aries — Day 43
**Theme**: Temper your fire
**Philosopher**: Musonius Rufus
**Source**: Lectures, 6

## Quote
> "If you pursue the good, you will become good. If you pursue the bad, you will become bad."

## Application
For Aries, whose element drives you to lead with passion, this wisdom cuts through the noise. Let Musonius Rufus's words ground your natural fire — the lesson isn't in what you do, but in how you choose to see it. This week, let this truth settle into your daily rhythm.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Aries, notice where you're seeking approval — and give it to yourself instead.
