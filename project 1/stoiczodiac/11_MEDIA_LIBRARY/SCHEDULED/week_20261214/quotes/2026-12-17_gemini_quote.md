# ♊ Gemini — Day 123
**Theme**: The focused mind
**Philosopher**: Seneca
**Source**: Letters from a Stoic, LXXI

## Quote
> "If a man knows not to which port he sails, no wind is favorable."

## Application
Gemini energy is magnetic — but without intention, it scatters. Seneca offers a tether: a reminder that your greatest work begins inward. Let this quote sit with you today. Let it shape one decision.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Gemini, notice where you're seeking approval — and give it to yourself instead.
