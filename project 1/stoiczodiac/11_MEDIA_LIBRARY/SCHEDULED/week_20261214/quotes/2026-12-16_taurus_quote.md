# ♉ Taurus — Day 122
**Theme**: Contentment with enough
**Philosopher**: Epictetus
**Source**: Discourses, 1.29

## Quote
> "Circumstances do not rise to meet our expectations. Events happen as they do. People behave as they are. Embrace what you actually get."

## Application
As a Taurus, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Epictetus reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Taurus, act as if your character — not your reputation — is the only thing that matters.
