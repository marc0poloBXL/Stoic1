# ♍ Virgo — Day 126
**Theme**: Order in all things
**Philosopher**: Marcus Aurelius
**Source**: Meditations, 7.8

## Quote
> "Never let the future disturb you. You will meet it, if you have to, with the same weapons of reason which arm you against the present."

## Application
For Virgo, whose element shapes how you meet the world. The lesson isn't in what you do, but in how you choose to see it. This week, let this truth settle into your daily rhythm.

## Daily Challenge
Today, Virgo, act as if your character — not your reputation — is the only thing that matters.
