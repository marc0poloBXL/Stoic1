# ♑ Capricorn — Day 88
**Theme**: Time worthy steward
**Philosopher**: Zeno
**Source**: Zeno of Citium

## Quote
> "Worthwhile things take time. Do not expect to harvest what you have not planted."

## Application
As a Capricorn, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Zeno reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Capricorn, try this: when frustration rises, take three breaths before speaking.
