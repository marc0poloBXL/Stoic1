# ♊ Gemini — Day 147
**Theme**: The coherent self
**Philosopher**: Zeno
**Source**: Zeno of Citium

## Quote
> "Fate guides the willing, but drags the unwilling."

## Application
As a Gemini, your strength can become your shadow if left unchecked. Zeno reminds you that real power isn't in force — it's in the quiet mastery of your own mind. Carry this into your day like a compass.

## Daily Challenge
Gemini, before noon, write down one fear. Then ask: is this real, or imagined?
