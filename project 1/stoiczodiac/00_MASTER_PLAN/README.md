## 📌 The Core Concept

A faceless Instagram page that bridges **Stoic wisdom** (Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus) with **zodiac archetypes** (Aries–Pisces). Each post answers:

> *"What would a Stoic sage tell your zodiac sign today?"*

This is a **high-engagement, low-competition niche intersection**:
- **Stoicism** audience: ~15M+ on IG (self-improvement, discipline, mental strength)
- **Astrology** audience: ~50M+ on IG (identity, personal growth, spirituality)
- **Overlap**: People seeking meaning, daily guidance, and personal development
