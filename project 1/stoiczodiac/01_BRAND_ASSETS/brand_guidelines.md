## 🔤 Typography

- **Headlines**: Cormorant Garamond (serif, classical/stoic feel)
- **Body**: Raleway (sans-serif, clean/readable)
- **Quotes**: Playfair Display (italic, elegant)
- **Fallback**: Georgia / Helvetica

### Text Hierarchy

| Element | Font | Size (Canva) | Style |
|---------|------|-------------|-------|
| Quote | Playfair Display Italic | 48–64pt | Italic, Gold |
| Sign label | Cormorant Garamond | 36pt | Bold, sign color |
| Philosopher name | Raleway | 20pt | Light, White |
| Body text | Raleway | 16–18pt | Regular, White |
| Call to action | Cormorant Garamond | 24pt | Bold, Gold |

## 🗣️ Brand Voice

### Tone
- **Wise** but **accessible** — never academic
- **Direct** but **gentle** — never harsh
- **Ancient** but **relevant** — always applicable to modern life

### Language Rules
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| "You have the power to choose" | "One must choose virtue" |
| "Aries, your fire needs direction" | "Aries, you are impulsive" |
| "Seneca would tell you…" | "According to historical records…" |
| "Your sign's path today" | "Below is the astrological forecast" |
| Use "you" | Use "one" or "the reader" |
| Ancient wisdom, modern language | Ancient wisdom, ancient language |

### Caption Structure
1. **Hook** (1 line): "♈ Aries — your Stoic lesson today."
2. **Quote** (1–2 lines): "The happiness of your life depends upon the quality of your thoughts." — Marcus Aurelius
3. **Application** (2–3 lines): Apply this to your sign's specific strength/weakness.
4. **Challenge** (1 line): "Today: [one actionable thing]."
5. **Call to action** (1 line): "Save this for later. Follow @stoiczodiac for daily wisdom."
6. **Hashtags** (8–12): Mix of broad + niche + branded
